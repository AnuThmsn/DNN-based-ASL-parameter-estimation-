# Initial Technical Audit: Four-PLD ASL-MRI Parameter Estimation Pipeline

**Document Location:** `experiments_unet_4pld/audit/initial_audit.md`  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Subject:** Comprehensive diagnosis of simulator mechanics, noise models, baseline estimators, benchmark fairness, and U-Net training pipeline.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit examines the existing codebase across `data_gen.py`, `reference_baselines.yaml`, `scripts/exploratory/`, and `experiments_unet_4pld/` to identify the root causes of inconsistencies between neural network parameter estimation, historical baseline reference values, and classical estimators (Bayesian and Non-Linear Least Squares).

We identify **four critical scientific and implementation discrepancies**:
1. **The Simulator A Sum-of-Ricians Factor-of-Two Discrepancy:** The repository simulator adds two magnitude signals (`mc + ml`), resulting in an expected observation that is approximately $2 \times \Delta M$. Historical baselines that assumed physical $\Delta M$ experienced a 100% scale mismatch.
2. **Noise Variance Mismatch in the Bayesian Likelihood:** In `X = mc + ml`, the variance of the combined noise is $2\sigma^2$ ($\text{std} = \sqrt{2}\sigma$), but previous Bayesian estimators evaluated likelihoods using $\sigma^2$, leading to overconfidence and degraded posterior estimates.
3. **Sample-Size Asymmetry in NLLS Benchmarking:** NLLS was previously evaluated on 15 phantoms due to single-thread CPU execution time, whereas U-Net and Bayesian estimators were evaluated on 100 phantoms.
4. **Historical 0D Literature Baselines vs Spatial Phantoms:** The numbers in `reference_baselines.yaml` originated from 0D single-voxel simulations in earlier literature, which cannot be directly compared against spatially correlated 2D slice phantoms without explicit harmonization.

## 2. Issue Breakdown & Technical Diagnosis

### Issue 1: Simulator Formulation & Expected Signal Scale
- **Location:** `data_gen.py` (lines 56–66) & `experiments_unet_4pld/generate_spatial_data.py` (lines 142–156).
- **Description:**
  $$\text{sig} = \text{compute\_signals\_vec}(\text{cbf}, \text{att}) \times \text{SCALE}$$
  $$M_c = \sqrt{(\text{sig} + e_1)^2 + e_2^2}, \quad M_l = \sqrt{(\text{sig} + e_3)^2 + e_4^2}$$
  $$X = M_c + M_l$$
  In physical ASL, control and label images satisfy $M_c = M_0$ and $M_l = M_0 - \Delta M$, and the perfusion-weighted subtraction is $\Delta M = M_c - M_l$. In `data_gen.py`, both $M_c$ and $M_l$ are simulated around $\text{sig} = \Delta M$ and added. For high SNR, $\mathbb{E}[X] \approx 2 \cdot \text{sig}$.
- **Scientific Impact:** Any estimator (NLLS or Bayesian) that inverts $X$ assuming $\mathbb{E}[X] = \text{sig}$ suffers a $2\times$ amplitude mismatch, causing severe parameter bias (CBF doubled or clamped to boundary).
- **Required Correction:** For the primary benchmark adhering to the repository's established Simulator A, the estimator forward model must explicitly model the observation expectation $\mathbb{E}[X] = 2 \cdot \text{compute\_signals\_vec}(\text{cbf}, \text{att}) \times \text{SCALE}$. Furthermore, the physical subtraction model ($\Delta M = M_c - M_l$) must be documented as an alternative physical formulation.
- **Verification Test:** Unit test `test_signal_expectation` verifying that noiseless synthetic signals generated by the simulator match $2.0 \times \text{compute\_signals\_vec}$ to within $\epsilon < 10^{-4}$.

### Issue 2: Noise Variance Mismatch in Classical Likelihoods
- **Location:** `experiments_unet_4pld/evaluate.py` (lines 240–265).
- **Description:**
  In `data_gen.py`, independent Gaussian noise with standard deviation $\sigma$ is added to the real and imaginary components of both $M_c$ and $M_l$. For moderate to high SNR, $X = M_c + M_l \approx 2 \cdot \text{sig} + (e_1 + e_3)$, where $e_1, e_3 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.
  The variance of the sum is $\text{Var}(e_1 + e_3) = \sigma^2 + \sigma^2 = 2\sigma^2$, corresponding to an effective noise standard deviation of $\sigma_{eff} = \sqrt{2}\sigma$.
  In `evaluate.py`, the Bayesian likelihood used $\sigma = \text{SIG\_REF\_SC} / \text{SNR}$, underestimating the noise variance by a factor of 2.
- **Scientific Impact:** Underestimating the noise variance sharpens the Gaussian likelihood excessively, causing the Bayesian posterior to overfit noise fluctuations rather than marginalizing smoothly.
- **Required Correction:** Set the likelihood variance to $\sigma_{eff}^2 = 2\sigma^2$ (i.e., $\sigma_{eff} = \sqrt{2} \cdot \text{SIG\_REF\_SC} / \text{SNR}$).
- **Verification Test:** Unit test `test_noise_variance` verifying empirical sample variance of simulated noise across 50,000 voxels against the theoretical $2\sigma^2$.

### Issue 3: Evaluation Sample Size & Benchmark Asymmetry
- **Location:** `experiments_unet_4pld/evaluate.py` (lines 271–308).
- **Description:**
  NLLS optimization was capped at `max_phantoms = 15` due to single-thread CPU execution time, whereas U-Net, Point-wise DNN, and Bayesian estimators were evaluated on all 100 test phantoms.
- **Scientific Impact:** Comparing 15 phantoms against 100 phantoms violates test-set parity. Test metrics could be biased by phantom difficulty variation.
- **Required Correction:**
  1. Optimize the Bayesian estimator via PyTorch GPU tensor operations ($>100\times$ speedup, allowing high grid resolution $100 \times 100$).
  2. Implement an explicitly declared, harmonized test set where **every method** (U-Net, Point-wise DNN, Bayesian, NLLS) is evaluated on the exact same declared phantoms.
  3. Also report the full 100-phantom test set for methods evaluated at scale.
- **Verification Test:** Automated assertion checking that `len(y_true) == len(y_pred)` across all compared methods in the harmonized benchmark.

### Issue 4: Historical 0D Literature Baselines vs 2D Spatial Phantoms
- **Location:** `reference_baselines.yaml` and `baseline_comparison.csv`.
- **Description:**
  The numbers in `reference_baselines.yaml` (Bayesian CBF RMSE 5.03, ATT RMSE 0.0826 at SNR 10; NLLS CBF RMSE 2.982, ATT RMSE 0.5062 at SNR 10) are fixed literature reference values obtained on 0D single-voxel simulations without spatial correlation.
- **Scientific Impact:** Comparing a spatial U-Net (which leverages 2D neighborhood denoising) against 0D literature numbers without harmonized comparison conflates spatial filtering benefits with model capacity.
- **Required Correction:**
  1. Retain historical literature reference values in a clearly labeled external reference section.
  2. Include a **Point-wise DNN** trained and tested on the exact same spatial phantoms (voxel-wise) to isolate the precise gain provided by spatial convolution versus point-wise nonlinear fitting.
- **Verification Test:** Paired statistical comparison between U-Net and Point-wise DNN on identical test phantoms.

---

### Issue 5: Seed Diversity for Full Benchmark
- **Location:** `experiments_unet_4pld/config.json`.
- **Description:**
  The preliminary run used seeds `[42, 43, 44]`. The project's canonical audit seeds across earlier rounds are `[42, 123, 2024]`.
- **Scientific Impact:** Testing seeds spanning multiple orders of magnitude (`42`, `123`, `2024`) provides stronger protection against pseudo-random generator cycle correlation.
- **Required Correction:** Adopt canonical seeds `[42, 123, 2024]` for the definitive benchmark.
- **Verification Test:** Unit test checking seed uniqueness and deterministic reproduction.
