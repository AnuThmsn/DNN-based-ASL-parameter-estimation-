## 4. Forward Physics Model and Noise Verification

We audited the forward equations in [`data_gen.py`](file:///d:/fiinal%20year%20project/dnn%20%20for%20%20ASL%20parameter%20estimation/data_gen.py) and executed [`experiments_unet_4pld/tests/test_forward_model_and_noise.py`](file:///d:/fiinal%20year%20project/dnn%20%20for%20%20ASL%20parameter%20estimation/experiments_unet_4pld/tests/test_forward_model_and_noise.py):

### The Buxton Two-Compartment Model
$$\Delta M(t) = 2 \alpha \beta T_{1t} \left(\frac{1}{\lambda}\right) f \cdot e^{-\Delta / T_{1a}} \left[ e^{-\max(t - \Delta, 0)/T_{1t}} - e^{-\max(\tau + t - \Delta, 0)/T_{1t}} \right]$$
- $\text{PLDs} = [1.525, 2.025, 2.525, 3.025]$ s, $\tau = 1.8$ s.
- $T_{1a} = 1.66$ s, $T_{1t} = 1.20$ s, $\alpha = 0.85$, $\beta = 0.75$, $\lambda = 0.90$.
- Units: CBF in ml/100g/min ($f = \text{CBF} / (6000 \cdot \lambda)$ s$^{-1}$), ATT in seconds.
- Scale: $\text{SCALE} = 100,000.0$.
- Linearity and non-negativity: Passed ($CBF=0 \implies \Delta M = 0$, $2\times CBF \implies 2\times \Delta M$).

### Simulator A Observation & Sum-of-Ricians Mechanics
In `data_gen.py`, control ($M_c$) and label ($M_l$) magnitudes are both simulated around $\text{sig} = \Delta M \cdot \text{SCALE}$ with orthogonal Gaussian noise $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$:
$$M_c = \sqrt{(\text{sig} + e_1)^2 + e_2^2}, \quad M_l = \sqrt{(\text{sig} + e_3)^2 + e_4^2}, \quad X = M_c + M_l$$
1. **Mathematical Expectation:** For moderate to high SNR ($\text{sig}/\sigma \ge 5$), the Rician distribution converges to Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\text{sig}, \sigma^2)$. Thus:
   $$\mathbb{E}[X] = \mathbb{E}[M_c] + \mathbb{E}[M_l] \approx 2 \cdot \text{sig} = 2 \cdot \Delta M \cdot \text{SCALE}$$
   Empirical test ($150,000$ samples, SNR=15): Expected = 492.20, Measured Mean = 492.21 (Error $< 0.01\%$).
2. **Noise Variance:** Independent addition gives:
   $$\text{Var}(X) = \text{Var}(M_c) + \text{Var}(M_l) \approx \sigma^2 + \sigma^2 = 2\sigma^2 \implies \sigma_{eff} = \sqrt{2}\sigma = \sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{\text{SIG\_REF\_SC}}{\text{SNR}}$$
   Empirical test: Expected Variance = 995.3, Measured Variance = 997.1 (Error $< 0.2\%$).
3. **CLT Normality:** For SNR $\ge 10$, skewness is $< 0.05$ and excess kurtosis is $< 0.10$, confirming that a Gaussian likelihood with $\sigma_{eff} = \sqrt{2}\sigma$ is a rigorous Bayesian representation.

### Physical Mechanism:
1. **$P_4$ Redundancy:** By $t = 3.025$ s, magnetic relaxation ($T_{1t} = 1.2$ s) has decayed the difference signal toward noise. Dropping $P_4$ loses minimal information.
2. **$P_1$ Criticality:** Most physiological arrival times occur between $0.8$ and $1.4$ s. Without $P_1$, bolus arrival is completely unobserved, destroying transit time identifiability.

## 8. Spatial Generalization Stress-Testing & Failure Analysis

Evaluated against four unseen test cases ($N = 10$ phantoms each, SNR = 12):

| Pathology Case | Description | U-Net CBF RMSE | DNN CBF RMSE | Bayes CBF RMSE | U-Net ATT RMSE | DNN ATT RMSE | Bayes ATT RMSE | U-Net CBF SSIM |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Focal Infarct** | Sharp stroke lesion (CBF=5, ATT=2.8s) | 7.704 | **1.871** | 3.246 | 0.4335 | **0.2094** | 0.2878 | 0.8804 |
| **Hydrocephalus** | $3\times$ enlarged ventricles, thin cortex | 5.152 | **1.859** | 3.937 | 0.2632 | **0.0972** | 0.2651 | 0.9422 |
| **Step Discontinuity** | Unsmoothed checkerboard cortex | 5.888 | 3.519 | **2.038** | 0.3490 | 0.2621 | **0.2570** | 0.9187 |
| **Watershed Ischemia** | Linear transit delay gradient | 3.436 | **3.189** | 3.629 | **0.2659** | 0.2682 | 0.2892 | 0.9093 |

### Root Cause Analysis of Generalization Limits:
- **Spatial Inductive Bias Oversmoothing:** U-Net operates with a 4-level convolutional encoder-decoder, learning smooth spatial priors. When an abrupt step change occurs over 1 pixel (focal infarct boundary or step pattern), convolutions blur across the edge, creating a boundary transition zone with high squared error.
- **Single-Voxel Estimators:** Point-wise DNN and Bayesian estimators make zero spatial continuity assumptions. At voxels inside the lesion, they predict low CBF based strictly on local signal intensity, avoiding boundary spillover.
- **Watershed Delay:** When the transition is smooth (linear gradient), U-Net performance matches Point-wise DNN (ATT RMSE 0.2659 vs 0.2682 s).
